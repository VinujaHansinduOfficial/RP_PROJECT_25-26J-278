# Student Mental Health Mobile App

## Overview of the Project

The **Student Mental Health Mobile App** is a comprehensive AI-powered system designed to proactively monitor, predict, and support the mental well-being of university students. Unlike traditional reactive mental-health solutions, this project focuses on **early detection and forecasting** of stress, anxiety, depression, and burnout using continuous, non-intrusive data collection.

The system integrates multiple intelligent modules—including text analysis, behavioral monitoring, facial emotion recognition, and on-device AI summarization—to generate personalized insights and interventions. Privacy and ethics are core design principles, with most intelligence executed **directly on the user’s device** and clinician feedback incorporated through a human-in-the-loop approach.

## Project Overview

## Project Objectives

* Develop an early mental-health prediction system that identifies risk trends before symptoms escalate.
* Provide real-time emotion detection and proactive, personalized interventions.
* Enable privacy-preserving, on-device AI summarization of mental-health signals.
* Detect student burnout early and recommend ML-driven support strategies.
* Support universities and counseling centers with scalable, data-driven mental-health tools.

---

## Problem Statement

University students increasingly face academic pressure, emotional stress, and burnout. Traditional mental-health support systems are reactive, relying on self-report questionnaires or delayed counseling sessions. These methods often fail to detect early warning signs and may be intrusive or inaccessible. There is a need for a proactive, non-intrusive, privacy-safe system that continuously monitors mental well-being and delivers timely support.

## Accessibility & Inclusive Support

### Visual Impairment Support

* Screen-reader compatible UI design
* Voice-based interactions and audio feedback
* High-contrast themes and adjustable text sizes

### Hearing Impairment Support

* Text-based notifications and alerts
* Visual emotion and stress indicators
* Captioned and text-based intervention guidance

### Cognitive and Learning Difficulties Support

* Simple, distraction-free interface
* Clear summaries instead of raw analytics
* Personalized recommendations delivered in small, manageable steps

## Technologies Used

* **Frontend**: Flutter 
* **Backend**: Python
* **Machine Learning & AI**:

  * Facial emotion recognition models

* **On-Device AI**: Lightweight models for local inference and summarization
* **Security & Privacy**:

  * On-device data processing
  * Secure encryption for sensitive data

## System Architecture (High-Level)

**1. User Layer (Mobile App)**

* Student text input (journals, mood logs)
* Behavioral signals (usage patterns, engagement)
* Facial expressions and optional physiological signals
* Accessibility support (voice, text, visual aids)



**2. On-Device AI Layer**

* Text emotion & burnout analysis (NLP)
* Early mental-health prediction models (LSTM, TCN)
* Facial emotion detection models
* Lightweight summarization engine
* Local risk scoring and alert generation


**3. Intelligence & Decision Layer**

* Multimodal data fusion
* Temporal forecasting of mental-health risk
* Personalized intervention selection
* Explainable AI outputs for user trust



**4. Intervention Layer**

* Real-time alerts and recommendations
* Personalized coping strategies
* Stress and burnout prevention guidance
* Adaptive intervention timing and intensity



**5. Clinician & Institutional Layer (Optional)**

* Secure dashboards for counselors
* Human-in-the-loop feedback and validation
* Aggregated, anonymized insights for universities



**6. Security & Privacy Layer (Cross-Cutting)**

* On-device processing by default
* Encrypted data handling
* User-controlled consent and data sharing
